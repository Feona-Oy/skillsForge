Every ring is a level of difficulty. Every wedge is a branch. Each node is a build a learner defends to peers while an instructor records what they actually did. Select a node to read its specification.

Node index

Assessment of record is the instructor observation, not the peer verdict. The peer defense generates its own evidence on the evaluator: whether they can name a criterion, justify with evidence, and disagree without deflating the builder. Divergence between the two is the inter-rater reliability signal. No node is gated — completing one suggests the next.
